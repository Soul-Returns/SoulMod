## New
- **Corpse locator** shares located corpses with party members in the same mineshaft; turn off **Share with party** to opt out
- **Corpse locator** marks any corpse you can see right away, even before the ding or without the Organ Donor accessory
- **Corpse locator** names the type of a corpse you have not seen when the tab list leaves only one type

## Improved
- **Log mineshaft visits** now includes the mineshaft variant and the positions of corpses you saw, to complete the spot list

## Fixed
- **Corpse locator** did nothing in the Ruby crystal mineshaft
- **Corpse locator** could drop a located corpse and let its ding play again while you stood closer to another one
