## New
- **Experimentation Table**: all three games in Soul's window, with solvers, misclick blocking and a rare card warning
- **Search everywhere**: one search over your island chests and storage pages, from anywhere on SkyBlock
- **Search everywhere**: a Forge tab (`Ctrl+Tab`) plans a forge recipe down to what you already own
- **Storage overlay**: your Ender Chest and backpacks on one searchable wall; click a page to open it
- **Override Skyblocker storage** is on by default while the overlay is enabled; turn it off to keep Skyblocker's
- **Commissions HUD**: shows your commissions with their progress on screen, DONE in green
- **Highlight completed commissions**: finished commissions get a green mark in the Commissions menu
- **Hide Powder Coating particles**: hides the dust around players wearing coated Divan armor

## Improved
- **Island chests**: blue slot highlights mark matching stacks; shared co-op chests sync anywhere on SkyBlock
- **Log mineshaft visits**: `/soul mineshaft` and the Profile Viewer now show your odds per profile

## Fixed
- **Storage overlay** keeps clear of the screen edge and recipe viewer panels, so they no longer cover it
- Tracker panels now draw under recipe viewer item lists in your inventory instead of over them
