## New
- **Friends & Contests**: add Soul friends, see who is online and where, and run contests with them; open with `/soul friends`
- **Friends & Contests**: the first contest type is most Crystal Nucleus profit over a set number of runs, with a live leaderboard and HUD
- **Friends & Contests**: runs upload by themselves and are priced by Soul's server; a results reveal at the end, past contests under History
- **Friends can see where I am**: on by default; turn it off to show friends only that you are online
- **Profile Viewer** rebuilt with 17 sections, a player model in armor, market value and the Heart of the Mountain tree
- **Profile Viewer** opens with `/sp <name>` and searches every container at once; `/spv` is gone
- **Use /pv**: lets `/pv` open Soul's Profile Viewer ahead of other mods; off by default
- **Corpse locator**: points you to Frozen Corpses from the Organ Donor ding and marks any corpse you can see
- **Corpse locator** shares located corpses with your party and mutes the ding for them; **Share with party** opts out
- **Vanguard guild call**: posts in guild chat when you find a Vanguard mineshaft and invites whoever answers "me"
- **Search everywhere** also finds items in your sacks; turn off **Search sacks** to opt out
- **Highlight active pet**: marks the summoned pet in the Pets menu

## Improved
- **Search everywhere**: the Forge tab lists compacted forms, counts down running slots and shows head skins
- **Kill Combo overlay** shows Combat Wisdom and coins per kill and learns your Grandma Wolf's timers
- **Log mineshaft visits** also records the mineshaft variant and corpse positions you saw
- Party tracking corrects itself from Hypixel's party info after every change
- Items in Soul's screens and HUDs are drawn sharp at every UI scale
- New versions are offered moments after release instead of at the next hourly check

## Fixed
- **Pet HUD** shows the right pet name and level right after you summon a pet
- **Crystal Nucleus tracker** no longer counts other mods' chat lines, such as run profit, as loot
- Party alerts and party sharing missed members who joined through Party Finder or kept ones who had left

## Removed
- **Kill Combo overlay**: the Combat Exp line is gone; your Magic Find choice carries over to Combat Wisdom
