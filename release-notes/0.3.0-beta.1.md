## New
- **Terminal solver**: Goldor's six terminals in F7/M7 open in a clear Soul window showing what to click
- **Device solvers**: Simon Says, Lights, Arrow Align and Sharpshooter show the next button, lever, arrow or target
- **Boss timers**: pad tick, the Purple crush cue, crush window and Storm's phases at your crosshair in F7/M7
- **Hide Soulweaver skulls**: stops drawing the Haunted Skulls around you and your party in dungeons
- **Hide fire**: hide the fire on your screen and the flames on burning players and mobs
- The terminal and device solvers can block wrong clicks, so a misclick doesn't cost you the terminal or device

## Fixed
- **Pet HUD** now follows your pet when you switch loadouts
- **Dungeon map**: other players' markers sit on their real position instead of about two blocks north-west
