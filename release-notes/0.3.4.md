## New
- **Scoreboard**: SkyBlock's sidebar drawn by Soul as a HUD you can move and style; off by default
- **Scoreboard** has its own setup screen: open it with **Set up** on the card or `/soul scoreboard`
- **Scoreboard**: about 140 lines to pick from, including tab list widgets and Soul's own trackers
- **Scoreboard**: your own text rows with colors and live values, an editable header and footer, colors per line
- **Scoreboard**: **Different setups per activity** gives Dungeons, Kuudra and others their own lines
- **Scoreboard** offers once to import your Custom Scoreboard setup
- **Scoreboard**: border in one color or as a moving two-color gradient

## Improved
- **Chroma** is one diagonal rainbow across the whole screen, in softer colors; every chroma color lines up
- **Chroma** speed and motion are set under Settings > Interface; a color's own chroma speed no longer applies
- Borders and rounded corners are drawn the same way everywhere in Soul, and follow each other at the corners

## Fixed
- REI no longer shows tooltips or takes clicks through **SkyBlock info** and **Inventory buttons**
- Colored text keeps its spaces; values no longer stick to their labels
- **Chronomatron** no longer lights up a tile you clicked correctly, so the next clicks stand out
