## New
- **Vanguard guild call**: posts in guild chat when you find a Vanguard mineshaft and invites every guild member who answers "me"
- **Search everywhere** also finds items in your sacks; turn off **Search sacks** to opt out

## Improved
- **Corpse locator**: **Mute the ding** now silences every Organ Donor ding in mineshafts with known corpse spots
- **Corpse locator** removes a waypoint when later dings or a close look show no corpse there
- **Search everywhere**: Forge slots count down to the time The Forge shows and turn green when ready
- Party tracking corrects itself from Hypixel's party info after every change

## Fixed
- **Corpse locator** could show one waypoint more than corpses left in the mineshaft
- **Corpse locator** could mark the wrong corpse as looted when two were close together
- **Search everywhere**: the Forge slot tooltip flickered and its time ignored Quick Forge
- Party alerts and party sharing missed members who joined through Party Finder or kept ones who had left
