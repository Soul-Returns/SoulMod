## New
- **Terminal solver**: all six F7/M7 terminals show what to click in a Soul window or the normal menu
- **Device solvers**: highlights for Simon Says, Lights, Arrow Align and Sharpshooter in F7/M7
- **Terminal solver** and **Device solvers** can block wrong clicks; Sharpshooter only highlights
- **Leap menu**: four teammate tiles with class corners, locations and number keys 1-4
- **Leap menu**: hover a teammate to mark them on the undimmed **Dungeon map**
- **Archer roles**: objective markers and advice on who to leap to through F7/M7 boss fights
- **Island chests**: search remembered items and book enchantments, then locate matching chests
- **Island chests**: co-op sharing starts on when enabled; existing sharing choices stay unchanged
- **Island chests**: press `K` to search or return from locating, and `Esc` to cancel; the key is editable
- **Boss timers**: pad tick, crush window, Storm's phases and Goldor's death tick at your crosshair
- **Boss timers**: countdown until Goldor's terminals open
- **Dragon spawn countdown**: M7 dragon timers at their spawns and above your crosshair
- **Section not open warning**: warns while you stand in a Goldor section that has not begun
- **Hide Soulweaver skulls**: hide Haunted Skulls around you and your party in dungeons
- **Hide fire**: separately hide flames on your screen and on burning players and mobs

## Improved
- **Dungeon map**: default class colors match the **Leap menu**; your custom colors stay unchanged
- **Updates**: the update window opens before the main menu; update notices appear as you join
- **Start**: What's new stays listed for the whole version; seen lines lose only their NEW highlight
- Safer login to Soul services; dungeon secret sharing is private to your run's teammates

## Fixed
- **Pet HUD** follows your active pet when you switch loadouts
- **Dungeon map**: teammate markers no longer sit about two blocks north-west of their real position
- **Updates**: automatic restart prompts wait for 60 idle seconds, never in Dungeons or Kuudra
