## New
- **Auto size** button in Settings > Interface: picks the UI size that fits your monitor
- Updates show on the title screen, before you join a server, and ask once whether to turn on automatic updates

## Improved
- **UI scale** is now picked from your monitor on a new install; existing settings stay as they are
- The update window explains that you restart Minecraft to use the new version, or click Later to keep playing

## Fixed
- HUDs attached to a tracker panel now stay beside it while your inventory is open

## Removed
- **Highlight Hyperion**: the border around your Hyperion is gone, and its settings are removed from your config
